# Rogue Radar

> 🐱 **Custom** — built for this fork (not in the original firmware).

Two rogue-AP detectors in one app.

## Controls
- **short B** — switch Evil-Twin ↔ Beacon-Flood
- **A** — rescan / pause
- **hold B** — exit

## Modes
- **Evil-Twin scan** — flags SSIDs that are being advertised **both open and
  secured** at the same time, a classic sign of a fake "twin" of a real network.
- **Beacon-Flood / Karma** — promiscuously counts distinct APs per second; a
  sudden flood of new AP names is a beacon-spam / Karma attack.

Passive / listen-only.
